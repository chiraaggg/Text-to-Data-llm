# ShopEasy DataBot 🛒

**Natural language analytics** for a demo e-commerce platform. Ask questions in plain English — the AI writes SQL, runs it, and explains the results.

## Demo Dataset
- **900 orders** across 6 months
- **150 customers** across 15 Indian cities  
- **56 products** in 8 categories (Fruits, Dairy, Snacks, Beverages, Meat, Frozen, Personal Care, Household)
- Payments via UPI, Card, Cash, Wallet

## Try asking:
- *"What's the total revenue this month?"*
- *"Which product category sells the most?"*
- *"Plot daily orders for the last 30 days"*
- *"Who are the top 5 customers by spend?"*
- *"What's the average order value by city?"*

> Built with **LangChain** · **OpenAI GPT-4o** · **Chainlit** · **SQLite**
